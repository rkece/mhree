A SPECIAL LETTER FROM MY HEART

To the Closest Person to My Heart

You are, and will always be, the closest person to my heart in this whole universe. Out of everyone in the world, having you in my life means more to me than words can ever express. You know the real me, and you have always stood by me with pure understanding.

I have always admired you so deeply. Every single day, I wonder how you carry so much patience and gentle grace. There were countless times when I got angry, lost my cool, or was difficult to handle. But you never once treated me badly. You never answered anger with anger, and you never held a grudge. You always met my storms with a calm, gentle heart and pure peace that completely humbled me.

Sometimes my heart always searches for you wherever I am, and I just can't leave you alone. What makes you so special is how pure and innocent your spirit is. In a world full of noise, your heart is completely real, honest, and sweet beyond measure.

And from the deepest corner of my heart, I am truly sorry for whatever times I got angry, reacted harshly, or hurt you in any way. You never deserved any harsh words. No matter where life takes us, I promise you that I will always be right there with you, walking beside you through everything.

❝

When storms arose and patience broke, you stayed so calm and sweet,

You never answered fire with fire, nor treated me with heat.

My heart will always search for you, wherever paths may bend,

I'm sorry for the times I hurt, my promises I'll mend.

Through every joy and every cloud, I'll walk right by your side,

The closest person to my heart, my truest peace and guide.

❞
Forever thankful for you and always with you, Rakesh Kumar
